How I work

I plan the structure, write and revise the copy, and work product recommendations into practical styling ideas. I also write the headings, FAQs, calls to action, and image alt text.

For seasonal and color stories, I turn a visual theme into specific products, textures, and décor. For bridal content, I follow the reader’s experience, from the overnight celebration to the morning of the wedding.
