Paid Media Performance Diagnosis

SQL · R · Marketing Analytics

−14%Ad clicks, week of Aug 10 vs prior week
−20%Attributed purchases per click, same week
−48%Click volume vs Aug 3 baseline, by Aug 24

The question

I analyzed 13 complete weeks of Google Ads data to understand a drop in attributed purchases. Did the campaign get fewer clicks, fewer attributed purchases per click, or both?

How I analyzed it

SQL: I used CTEs to clean the weekly data, NULLIF() to handle zero denominators when calculating CPC and attributed purchases per click, and LAG() to flag weeks when spend rose but attributed purchases fell.

R: I indexed each metric to its August 3 value and plotted both on matching scales to compare trends without showing raw counts.

Chart

Findings & business implications

In the week of August 10, ad clicks fell 14% and attributed purchases per click fell 20% compared with the prior week. By August 24, attributed purchases per click had returned to roughly its August 3 level, while click volume remained 48% below that baseline.

I would next examine spend and CPC together, then compare device and geographic segments. This would help assess whether lower click volume reflected reduced spend, a shift toward more expensive traffic, or higher CPC within the same segments.
