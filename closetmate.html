ClosetMate: Turning User Preferences into Product Priorities

Survey Research · Conjoint Analysis · Sawtooth

39Survey responses
11Completed conjoint responses

Research question

Which features and pricing options should a wardrobe-management app prioritize for its first version?

My role & approach

I designed the survey questions and analyzed the research findings. Our team combined a survey with a Sawtooth conjoint study to examine concept interest, feature preferences, and pricing trade-offs.

Findings & product recommendation

Closet organization was the most preferred functionality among the options tested, while daily outfit planning ranked lower. Participants preferred a free basic version over paid subscriptions and valued saving time.

We recommended a simple, free MVP focused on organizing clothes and finding them quickly. We proposed further testing of AI styling and outfit planning before prioritizing them for development.

The research helped turn a broad app concept into a more focused set of product priorities.

These exploratory findings informed our prototype recommendations. The small sample did not establish market demand or predict adoption.
