User Research & Field Experimentation · Academic team project, UC San Diego

Campus Free Printing: Testing Researcher Presence Through Signing Behavior

Randomized Field Experiment · Fisher's Exact Test · R

147Students approached
+6.9 ptsSigning rate, three researchers vs one
p = 0.458Fisher’s exact test

Research question

Does having three researchers present, instead of one, change whether students sign a petition for free campus printing?

Approach

I helped collect data in person at UC San Diego’s Library Walk. Before each approach, we flipped a coin to assign one researcher or three.

In the three-researcher condition, one person gave the pitch and two stayed silent. We kept the lead researcher, petition, and script the same in both conditions, and recorded whether each student signed.

Results

ConditionStudents who signedSigning rate
One researcher16 of 6823.5%
Three researchers24 of 7930.4%

The three-researcher condition had a signing rate 6.9 percentage points higher. Fisher’s exact test showed this was not statistically significant (p = 0.458).

Interpretation & recommendation

We could not conclude that three researchers increased signing. We recommended collecting more evidence before adopting the more staff-intensive approach. With 147 students, the test may have been too small to detect a real difference. A non-significant result does not mean the two approaches work equally well.
